feat: add Motion Suite 2.0.0

Client-side Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11 with a motion blur for OpenGL
and VulkanMod, an ender pearl camera glide and a crystal PvP guard.

- Frame-blending blur with an adaptive mode and a ghost-free shader
- Pearl camera glide with separate first and third person speeds
- Crystal guard that tightens the camera and eases the blur
- Blur history reset on respawn, dimension change and teleport
- One config file, config/motionsuite.json, with clamped values
- Commands: /motionblur, /motioncamera, /motionsuite
